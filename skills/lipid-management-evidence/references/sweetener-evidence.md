# Sweetener / Non-Sugar Sweetener Evidence Bank (accessed 2026-08-21)

Condensed evidence for answering "有什么代糖推荐吗 / 这个健康吗 / 长期吃行不行".
Cite these sources, not search snippets.

## Key sources

| Source | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| WHO, *Use of non-sugar sweeteners: WHO guideline*, 2023-05-15 (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240073616) | Conditional recommendation: do NOT use NSS for weight control or NCD-risk reduction. Long-term observational data suggest possible ↑T2D/CVD/mortality risk, but confounded. Applies to all NSS (acesulfame K, aspartame, advantame, cyclamates, neotame, saccharin, sucralose, stevia); EXCLUDES diabetics as a group and excludes polyols/sugar alcohols (erythritol etc. are not NSS). WHO's actual ask: reduce overall diet sweetness. |
| SWEET study, Pang et al., *Nat Metab* 2025;7:2083–2098, doi:10.1038/s42255-025-01381-z | 1-year multicentre RCT (341 adults + 38 children, overweight/obesity): S&SE group maintained more weight loss at 1y (+1.6±0.7 kg vs sugar group, P=0.029), beneficial gut-microbiota shifts (↑SCFA/methane taxa), no adverse cardiometabolic-marker differences. Conclusion: prolonged S&SE use within a healthy diet is a safe obesity-management strategy. |
| McGlynn et al., *JAMA Netw Open* 2022;5(3):e222092, doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.2092 | SR+MA: replacing sugar-sweetened beverages with low-/no-calorie sweetened beverages → small improvements in body weight & cardiometabolic risk, no evidence of harm; similar direction of benefit as water substitution. This is the evidence FOR the user's 健怡可乐 pattern (350–700 ml/day logged in dietary-profile). |
| FDA memo 2023-06-15, evaluation of Witkowski et al., *Nat Med* 2023;29:710-718 (erythritol & MACE) | Observational association (highest-plasma-quartile MACE HR ~1.8–2.2); FDA: association ≠ causation, unmeasured confounders (e.g. renal function), erythritol is an endogenous metabolite — **no change to erythritol GRAS status**. Laxative threshold ~0.66–0.8 g/kg bw per occasion (for 68.8 kg user ≈ 45–55 g). EFSA beverage limit based on GI tolerance only. |
| NutriNet-Santé cohort (Debras et al., BMJ 2022;378:e071204; Diabetes Care 2023;46:1681-1690) | Observational: higher artificial-sweetener intake (mainly beverages) associated with ↑CVD and ↑T2D. Confounded by reverse causality — this is the weak-evidence layer the WHO conditional recommendation draws on. |

## Per-sweetener summary (for practical recommendations)

| Sweetener | Calories | Notes / evidence posture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Erythritol (赤藓糖醇) | ~0.2 kcal/g | Closest-to-sugar mouthfeel, no glycemic response, non-cariogenic; the Nat Med 2023 MACE signal is observational (FDA: not causal). Main real limit: laxative threshold (~45–55 g single occasion). |
| Stevia glycosides (甜菊糖苷) | 0 | Neutral glucose effects in RCTs; some aftertaste; WHO-classified NSS with neutral substitution evidence. |
| Monk fruit / mogrosides (罗汉果甜苷) | 0 | Natural botanical extract, 150–250× sucrose sweetness; clinical evidence still limited (PRISMA RCT review PMC12073669, 2025), animal safety data clean; pricier. |
| Sucralose (三氯蔗糖/蔗糖素) | 0 | 2025 small triple-blind RCT suggests possible glucose-homeostasis/gut-microbiota effects; large RCTs & SWEET show no signal; fine in low amounts as a blend booster. |
| Aspartame + acesulfame K | 0 | What 健怡可乐 uses; PKU contraindication for aspartame; JECFA ADI maintained. |
| Xylitol | ~2.4 kcal/g | Polyol; SEVERE toxicity to dogs — lock away from pets; laxative at dose. |

## Practical framing (lipid-focused profile)

1. Sweeteners do not touch the sat-fat budget (0 g sat) — safe on the lipid axis.
2. Their real role: sugar-reduction transition tool (WHO), NOT a weight-loss drug and NOT a substitute for fiber/fish/unsaturated-fat work.
3. An existing diet-cola pattern is evidence-supported (McGlynn 2022) — no need to switch for health reasons.
4. Home-use recommendation class: monk fruit or stevia+erythritol blends (e.g. 爱乐甜零卡糖 = erythritol + resistant dextrin + sucralose + stevia, labelled 0 kJ/100g; record such products under `concepts/foods/` when the user sends a label). Resistant dextrin is a soluble fiber (bonus).
5. Watch amounts, not kinds: single-occasion erythritol >45–55 g is the only realistic acute risk.

## Label note

Chinese 0-calorie sweetener labels may list 碳水 98.5g/100g but 能量 0 kJ — legal under GB28050 Q&A (erythritol energy coefficient 0). Explain, don't treat as inconsistency.
