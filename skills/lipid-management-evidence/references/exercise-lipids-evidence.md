# Exercise × Blood Lipids × Saturated Fat — Evidence Bank (curated 2026-09-13)

## Core answer pattern ("does exercise offset eating too much SFA?")

Two ledgers:
1. What exercise helps: postprandial TG excursion (acute, needs a RECENT bout);
   TG/HDL/lipoprotein profile (chronic, modest); energy balance.
2. What it cannot offset: dietary SFA → LDL-C (dose-dependent, independent
   lever). Never present exercise as earning extra SFA headroom; SFA targets
   apply on training days too.

## Key trials (verified via Europe PMC REST 2026-09-13)

1. Sacks FM et al. AHA Presidential Advisory. Circulation 2017;136:e1–e23. PMID 28620111.
   - RCTs: lowering SFA + replacing with PUFA → CVD events ≈ −30%; replacement
     lowers LDL-C; causality criteria strongly satisfied.
2. Hooper L et al. Cochrane Database Syst Rev 2020. PMID 32827219 (2022 update: 35029948).
   - 15 RCT / 56,675 participants / ≥24 months: reduced-SFA → combined CVD
     events RR 0.83 (0.70–0.98) = −17% (GRADE moderate); greater SFA reduction →
     greater cholesterol and event reduction; NNTB 56 over ~4 yr (primary prevention).
3. Smart NA et al. Sports Med 2025;55:67–78 (online 2024-09-27). PMID 39331324.
   - 148 RCT / 8,673 participants: exercise training improved ALL lipids
     modestly (3.5–11.7%): TC −5.90, LDL −7.22, TG −8.01, HDL +2.11,
     VLDL −3.85 mg/dL. Combined aerobic+resistance optimal. Per extra weekly
     aerobic session: TC −7.68 mg/dL.
4. Kraus WE et al. NEJM 2002;347:1483–92 (DREW). PMID 12421890.
   - Sedentary overweight dyslipidemic adults ~6–8 mo: improvements related to
     exercise AMOUNT, not intensity/fitness; high-amount group 11/11 lipoprotein
     variables improved.
5. Teeman CS et al. Nutr Metab (Lond) 2016;13:80. DOI 10.1186/s12986-016-0142-6 (OA).
   - Acute exercise substantially attenuates postprandial lipemia after high-fat
     meals; effect TRANSIENT — depends on recent bout (LPL activity / IMTG stores);
     chronic training without a recent bout does not protect PPL. Within-review
     example: Tsetsonis 1997 — trained vs untrained women after same 90-min
     exercise: ~30% lower lipemia in trained.
6. 中国血脂管理指南2023: lifestyle (diet + activity) is the base layer;
   replace saturated fat with unsaturated fat.

## Communication rules

- Exercise LDL effect ≈ −7.2 mg/dL (−0.19 mmol/L) average → "modest"; never
  frame exercise as an LDL strategy or as an offset for dietary SFA.
- For fatty meals/gatherings, the honest "exercise helps here" statement is the
  acute postprandial-lipemia attenuation (TG line), plus energy balance.
- No compensation-fasting narratives; pattern-level view; clinician for targets.
