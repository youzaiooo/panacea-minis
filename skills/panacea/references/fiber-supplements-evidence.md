# Fiber Supplements: Types, Evidence, and Lipid-Lowering Efficacy

> Evidence summary compiled 2026-07-30. Re-verify key citations before citing in user-facing answers.

## Quick Reference: Ranked by Evidence for LDL-C Lowering

| Type | Source | Daily effective dose | LDL-C reduction | Evidence strength | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Psyllium (车前子壳)** | Plantago ovata husk | 5–10 g | **5–10%** | ★★★★★ | FDA-approved health claim; most studied |
| **Oat β-glucan** | Oat/barley extract | ≥3 g | **5–8%** | ★★★★☆ | Need purified extract; whole oats have less |
| **Glucomannan (魔芋)** | Konjac root | 3–4 g | 3–5% | ★★★☆☆ | Very viscous; choking risk if insufficient water |
| **PHGG** | Partially hydrolyzed guar gum | 5 g | Weak | ★★☆☆☆ | Well tolerated; mainly for regularity |
| **Inulin / FOS (菊粉)** | Chicory root | 5–10 g | None direct | ★★☆☆☆ | Prebiotic; may cause gas/bloating |

## Key Evidence

### 2024 Comprehensive Review

**PMID 38659110** — *Int J Food Sci Nutr*

- "Certain dietary supplements including phytosterols, berberine, **viscous soluble dietary fibres**, garlic supplements, soy protein, specific probiotic strains, and certain polyphenol extracts could significantly reduce plasma total and LDL cholesterol levels by **3-25%** in hypercholesterolemic patients."
- "They tended to be more effective in reducing plasma LDL cholesterol level in hypercholesterolemic individuals than in normocholesterolemic individuals."

### Psyllium-Specific Evidence

**PMID 30153584 (2018)** — *Nutrition* (critical review)

- "The addition of a soluble fiber product, **most notably psyllium**, improves blood lipid profiles, particularly total and LDL cholesterol."

## Guidelines

- **ESC/EAS 2019 Dyslipidemia Guidelines**: "Increase dietary fibre" — Class IIa, Level A
- FDA health claim: soluble fiber from psyllium husk may reduce heart disease risk (≥7g/day)

## Practical Usage: Psyllium

| Parameter | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Starting dose | 3–5 g/day, split 1–2× |
| Target dose | 5–10 g/day |
| Liquid required | **≥250 ml per dose** — non-negotiable (choking risk) |
| Timing | With/after meals; separate from medications by ≥2 hours |
| Hydration | Additional 500+ ml water/day |

## Caveats

1. **Not a medication replacement.** For LDL-C >4.0 mmol/L, even 10% reduction leaves LDL-C far above targets.
2. **Drug interactions.** Separate from medications by ≥2 hours.
3. **Choking risk.** Psyllium absorbs water rapidly — insufficient liquid causes esophageal obstruction.
4. **Only viscous soluble fibers** (psyllium, β-glucan, glucomannan) lower cholesterol; insoluble fiber does not.
